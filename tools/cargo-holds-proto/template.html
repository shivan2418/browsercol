Cargo hold prototypes

Four ways to draw what a ship or wagon train carries, after the hold slots of the 1994 Mac Colonization, in browsercol's FreeCol art and parchment palette. Each shows the same four loads in the three places a hold appears. Switch with the bar below or the arrow keys.

The view today sends only the holds left (in the title), not the holds in all; the capacities here are hard-coded, and the real ones need the Engine to send them.
