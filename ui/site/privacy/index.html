Privacy

BrowserCol has no accounts and sets no cookies. This page says what leaves your browser when you visit the site or play, and where it goes.

Your games and settings

Your saved games and your preferences are kept in your browser's storage on your own device. They are not sent anywhere, unless you attach a save to a feedback report yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Page views

When you open the home page, this site's other pages or the game, your browser tells GoatCounter, a counter that does not track you, which page you opened, its title and the address of the page you came from. GoatCounter works out your browser, system and country from the request, and recognises a repeat visit for up to 8 hours without storing your IP address. It keeps counts, not a record of you, on servers in Finland and Germany. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

The counts only tell us how many people visit and play. No cookie is set and nothing is stored in your browser, and if a blocker stops the request, the site and the game work just the same.

Feedback reports

Send feedback, in the corner of the start screen or in the menu bar during a game, sends a report to BrowserCol's server, which runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. A report holds what you write, the game's build and your browser's name and version. If you leave them ticked, it also holds the game's logs, any error messages and a save of your current game. Your Reddit name or email is sent only if you fill it in, and is used only to ask you about your report.

Reports are private: they are read by whoever maintains BrowserCol and never published. A report is kept until it has been read, and deleted 180 days after that. The server's own logs hold no report content and no IP addresses, and are deleted after 30 days.

To have a report deleted sooner, send feedback again with the reference you were given (it looks like R-ABCDE) and ask. If you would rather not use the server at all, the feedback window can save the report as a file instead, for you to send however you like.

Hosting

The site and the game are served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when your browser fetches the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement. Everything else the game needs, its fonts, art and music included, comes from this site.

Questions

Use Send feedback in the game, or open an issue on GitHub (issues there are public).
